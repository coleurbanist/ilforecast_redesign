Note: Age and gender breakdowns on a precinct/ward level reflect mail and early vote center voters only — in-precinct voters cannot be linked to individual precincts or wards. In-precinct vote counts are derived from official Board of Elections precinct totals. Early Vote Centers (EVC) are where all in-person early voting takes place; approximately 21,000 election-day votes are also cast at Early Vote Centers and are counted here rather than as in-precinct votes. Notes on vote method coverage: The "Mail & EVC" figures also include approximately 5,600 grace-period, 1,600 provisional, and 1,400 nursing-home ballots — all non-in-precinct methods — which together represent about 3.6% of the Mail & EVC total. About 3,800 voters who requested a mail ballot ultimately voted in-precinct instead; these are correctly excluded from the Mail & EVC figures and counted as in-precinct votes.
